ACT I: THE PROBLEM
(S: Silent, the problem)

AHNYCCA [Holds up the device; a breath sound plays] Listen. This is a breath. [Heartbeat plays] And this is a heartbeat.

HARED The moment the exam ends, both are gone. No recording. No second opinion. Walang resibo.

JARRED We're Team Wheezards, and better care in the barangay starts with five letters: S-O-U-N-D.

AHNYCCA S is for Silent. In 2024, ischaemic heart disease was the leading cause of death in the Philippines: 134,074 deaths, according to the PSA.

HARED Pneumonia was fourth, with 47,260.

JARRED [Counting on fingers] Together, about one in every four deaths in the country.

ALL Heard once. Gone forever.

ACT II: THE ROOT CAUSE
(O: Out of reach, the root cause)

HARED O is for Out of reach. In a barangay, nobody reviews what the midwife heard.

JARRED And listening alone is a weak test: 37 percent sensitivity across 34 studies.

AHNYCCA Because everyone listens in a different place and describes it differently.

✂ HARED After diagnosis, pulmonary rehabilitation reached only 2.7 percent of COPD patients worldwide.

✂ JARRED [Wide eyes, to the audience] Two point seven?! Halos wala!

AHNYCCA We interviewed practitioners at health facilities in Puerto Princesa to see where the gaps are.

ALL Hearing is not enough. Keeping it is.

ACT III: THE SOLUTION
(U: Understand every site, the solution)

JARRED U is for Understand every site. Meet Resondi. [Holds up the device] An IoT-based, AI-assisted smart stethoscope with a monitoring app.

AHNYCCA Choose Lung Mode or Heart Mode. The app walks you to seven lung sites and five heart sites, and files every recording by site and time.

HARED The device saves to its own memory card and runs its own Wi-Fi, so it works without internet. The parts cost ₱2,775.

JARRED A fusion model hears each breath three ways: spectrogram, MFCC, and chromagram. Three opinions, one verdict.

AHNYCCA For the heart, one model finds each heartbeat and the heart rate, and another flags a possible murmur.

✂ HARED Lung Mode also guides breathing exercises, but only after a practitioner approves them.

HARED And the practitioner has the last word: confirm, modify, or repeat. [Shows the practitioner dashboard]

ALL Capture. Sort. Analyze. Review.

ACT IV: THE PROOF
(N: Numbers, the results)

AHNYCCA N is for Numbers. We tested every part, and we'll give you every number, including the bad ones.

HARED The device's signal was 13 decibels stronger than a regular stethoscope's, about four and a half times the amplitude.

JARRED 93 percent of recordings were rated good or acceptable, in a quiet room, a hospital ward, and a community area.

AHNYCCA Our first model said it was 71 percent accurate. [Pause] It wasn't.

HARED Its test shared patients with its training. It learned who was breathing, not what it heard.

JARRED So we rebuilt it, and tested every one of 126 patients with a model that had never heard them.

AHNYCCA The old design: 34 percent. Our fusion model: 40. Chance is 25, and the gain is real: p equals 0.008.

HARED On 230 children from a second database: 56 percent.

✂ JARRED It is best at crackles, 56 percent, and weakest at wheezing with crackles, 26.

JARRED For the heart, tested on 189 patients: heart rate off by only 3 beats per minute on average.

AHNYCCA It correctly cleared 97 percent of normal hearts, but found only two of every three murmurs. Our target was 80. We missed it.

✂ HARED With noise 20 decibels below the signal, the lung model dropped to chance.

✂ AHNYCCA And 27 users rated the earlier version 3.95 out of 5.

JARRED Hindi pa po siya magic.

ALL Every number, honestly reported.
