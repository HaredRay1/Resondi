# Resondi: final 3-minute pitch script

**Deck:** "RESONDI - DICT" (46 slides), https://www.canva.com/design/DAHXYk_04ZA/edit

The script runs about 430 words. At a slow, calm pace of 140 words a minute, that is about 3 minutes.

- **Pauses:** pause at every "/". Take a full breath at every new slide.
- **Clicking through:** slides 1–3, 8, 11, 13, 16, 22, 34, 36 and 44–46 have no speech; just click through them.
- **Animation builds:** slides 17–21 and 28–31 are builds of one slide. Keep talking while you click through them.

---

**[4 · Title]** *(0:00)*
Good morning, judges. / We are the Wheezards, from Puerto Princesa City National Science High School, / and this is Resondi: / an AI-assisted smart stethoscope that turns every chest exam into a record a doctor can review.

**[5–7 · Story]** *(0:12)*
Tuesday, nine a.m. / A rural health unit in southern Palawan. / A nurse hears crackles in a patient's right lower lung. / The doctor visits on Friday, / and all the doctor gets is a note: "Crackles, right lower lobe." / Is the patient getting better, or worse? / No one can tell, / because the sound is gone.

**[9–10 · Problem]** *(0:32)*
Heart disease is the Philippines' number one killer: 134,074 deaths in 2024. / Pneumonia took 47,260 more. / Together, one in four Filipino deaths. / Both are first checked the same way: by listening to the chest.

**[12 · Follow-up]** *(0:48)*
And diagnosis is one visit, / but recovery takes many. / Without a record, every visit starts from zero.

**[14–15 · Solution]** *(0:56)*
So we built Resondi. / It works in four steps. / Record, at a guided site, even offline. / Organise, every file tagged by site and time. / Analyse: our AI suggests a lung-sound pattern. / And confirm: / a licensed practitioner checks every result. / The AI supports the clinician; / it never replaces them.

**[17–21 · Features]** *(1:16)*
The app makes the exam repeatable: / seven lung sites including the trachea, / the four valve points plus Erb's point for the heart, / the same order every visit, / with placement guidance on screen.

**[23 · SDG]** *(1:28)*
That is why our priority is SDG 3: / better care at the primary level, / and the health workers who deliver it.

**[24–25 · Market]** *(1:36)*
Thirty thousand public facilities listen to chests every day. / We start with 65 in MIMAROPA: / 50 facilities and 15 schools.

**[28–32 · Traction]** *(1:45)*
And it is built and field-tested / in a quiet room, a hospital ward and a community area. / Resondi picked up 45 decibels against 32 on a conventional stethoscope: / 13 decibels stronger. / Health professionals rated 68 percent of its recordings good. / We are honest about our AI: / it matched the reference on 6 of 15 field recordings, / 40 percent, / and that is exactly why we are asking.

**[33 · Where we stand]** *(2:08)*
Both ends of the system work: / the device and the review platform. / The link between them, / and clinical validation, / are what this round funds.

**[35, 26–27 · Business model]** *(2:18)*
We earn three ways: / an 8,000-peso device at a 50 percent margin, / a 1,500-peso monthly subscription per facility, / and a 30,000-peso yearly school licence.

**[37 · Competition]** *(2:30)*
No alternative keeps the sound, / guides the site / and routes it to a doctor. / Our real moat is a growing library of confirmed Philippine recordings.

**[38 · Roadmap]** *(2:38)*
Six gated phases over 18 months, / first sales in month 15, / and the full seed recovered by month 35.

**[39 · Ask]** *(2:45)*
We are asking for 500,000 pesos, / released phase by phase, with no salaries drawn, / plus two RHU pilot sites, a hospital partner, and incubation.

**[40 · Why us]** *(2:54)*
I'm Ahnycca, our CEO, / with Hared, our CTO, / and Jarred, our COO.

**[41–43 · Close]** *(2:59)*
So, back to Tuesday. / The nurse records the same seven sites with Resondi. / On Friday, the doctor plays Tuesday's sound beside today's. / Every breath, every heartbeat carries information. / We make sure none of it is lost. / Thank you.

*(about 3:10)*

**If a judge asks about a number:**
- **Signal:** 45 vs 32 dB is an indicative margin, not a formal signal-to-noise ratio.
- **Field agreement:** 40% is 6 of 15. Chance is 25%, and p = 0.148, so it is a baseline, not a validated result.
- **Payback:** month 35 counts from funding. That is commercial month 21.
