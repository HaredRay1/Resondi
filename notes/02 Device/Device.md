# Device

**Version 2.0 (current):** ESP32 · INMP441 digital MEMS microphone (24-bit output stored as 16-bit) · 32 GB microSD
· 20 x 4 display · two buttons · 10,000 mAh battery · Wi-Fi access point (own page at `192.168.4.1`) · PLA enclosure,
173 x 80 x 70 mm.

**Field build used in the study:** electret microphone on a MAX4466 board and Bluetooth Low Energy instead of Wi-Fi.
No result was obtained on version 2.0.

**Recording:** 16 kHz, 16-bit mono WAV, 15 s per recording.

**Cost:** bill of materials ₱2,775; unit cost ₱4,000.

**Measured in the study:** about 45 dB vs 32 dB with a conventional stethoscope (indicative margins, not a formal
SNR); recordings 68% good, 25% acceptable, 7% poor; background noise 24 / 35 / 42 dB (quiet room / ward / community).
