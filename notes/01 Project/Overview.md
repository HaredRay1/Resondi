# Overview

- **What:** AI-assisted smart stethoscope + web platform for guided lung and heart recording. Screening aid with
  decision support; a licensed practitioner confirms every result.
- **Team Wheezards**, Puerto Princesa City National Science High School, Palawan.
  - Founders: Ahnycca Krish N. Malimban, Hared Ray A. Cabrera, Jarred Paul G. Llacuna.
  - Ahnycca is the sole author of both RSTF papers.
- **Current work:** the research plan and IMRaD paper for the Regional Science and Technology Fair (RSTF).
  The PSC XI business competition (won the regional round, 26 Oct 2026) is over.
- **Sites:** 7 lung (six lung fields + trachea), 5 heart (four valve points + Erb's point).
- **Getting recordings to the app:** microSD card carried by a person, or (since 14 Nov) a phone that joins the
  device's Wi-Fi and uploads through the app. The automatic gateway is **not built**.
