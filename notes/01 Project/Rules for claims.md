# Rules for claims

> [!danger] Never say
> - that the model is **validated**, that it **diagnoses**, or that it **works on the Resondi device**;
> - **71.1% / 83%** (the withdrawn model's leaked scores);
> - the leaky rows of the leakage demonstration (58.2%, 52.5%, 78.5%) as accuracy;
> - the murmur detector as **80%** (80% was the target; the result is 66.7%).

> [!tip] Say instead
> - Lung fusion network: **40.0% balanced accuracy** (95% CI 35.9-43.5) in 5-fold cross-validation on all 126
>   ICBHI patients; chance is 25%. +5.9 points over MFCC (p = 0.008). Children (SPRSound): about 56%.
> - Heart: heart rate within **3.0 BPM** on average; murmur sensitivity **66.7%**, specificity 97.1%.
> - All on public datasets, patient-wise; not on Resondi recordings.

- Use only numbers that are in the project files; if a fact is not recorded, write "not recorded".
- Personal data never goes into notes: no survey respondents' names or e-mails, no patient details, no phone
  numbers or e-mails from forms, no passwords, keys or tokens.
- Never edit names on signed certificates or forms.
