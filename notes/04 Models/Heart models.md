# Heart models

CirCor DigiScope, patient-wise split, 189 test patients (children and adolescents).

- S1 found 96.8%, S2 97.3%.
- **Heart rate:** mean error 3.0 BPM (CI 2.5-3.6); 85% within 5 BPM.
- **Murmur:** sensitivity **66.7%** (CI 50.0-81.8), **short of the 80% target**; specificity 97.1%; AUROC 0.96.
- Not tested on Resondi heart recordings. A cardiologist's blinded review of 25 recordings is pending (the answer
  key is private and stays out of this folder).
