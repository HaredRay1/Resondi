Bundles and onboarding offers

A facility buying a device and a subscription in the same procurement is making one decision, not two, and an LGU budget line is easier to defend as a single annual figure. One rule governs every discount: nothing comes out of what pays cloud operation and the device replacement reserve: ₱600 a month on Essential, ₱700 on Clinical, ₱1,200 on Network.

New facility, full Essential capability
Fourteen days or twenty-five guided examinations, whichever comes first, with no payment details taken. About ₱750 per facility. A free month would cost ₱1,500: the ₱900 of contribution plus the ₱600 of cloud and reserve still consumed.
Feature trial
Sixty days of Clinical features while paying Essential
Costs the ₱100 monthly difference in cloud allowance, twice. ₱200 per facility, once. Gives away capability, not revenue. It cannot be offered until the ₱38,000 mobile-application line has built those features.
Offers do not stack, and no trial turns into a charge: when one ends, the facility chooses a tier. Every price here is a proposal, not a validated price — no buyer has yet agreed to ₱2,900, and willingness to pay is one of the questions the pre-launch budget-holder interviews exist to answer.
