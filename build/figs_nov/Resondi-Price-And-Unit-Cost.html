Why the bridge line exists. ₱2,735 is the confirmed cost of the parts in the prototype. The bill of materials states that the battery protection and charging circuit are not included in that total, and a unit built to sell also needs connectors, strain relief, a finished enclosure and a spares allowance. Those sit in the ₱665 line. Confirm the battery protection and charging portion: if it is materially above ₱300, the ₱4,000 fully loaded figure needs revisiting before the ₱8,000 price does.
Schools pay a separate ₱30,000 annual teaching licence, ₱25,800 after platform cost. The device is profitable from the first production unit, so the subscription is a second margin stream rather than cover for a hardware loss.
